HOW PLAYOFFS WORK

Full Rules →
01
Top 4 teams from the standings make the playoffs. #5 and #6 miss out.
02
Seeding uses game wins — the same rule as the standings ranking.
03
#1 vs #4 on the left, #2 vs #3 on the right. Winners meet in the championship.
04
Matchups resolve on +/-. Once seeded, bracket matchups compare season point differentials. Higher +/- advances.
05
Why +/-? Cross-pairing teams may never have played each other, so W/L head-to-head doesn't exist. +/- always does.
06
No double counting. Playoff results don't add to stats — +/- is already tallied. Bracket is a live projection, locks after Week 3.
